This engineering dossier consolidates the architectural specifications, circuit topologies, safety systems, pin allocations, and component Bill of Materials for the **16S 100Ah (200A continuous) LiFePO4 Smart BMS Satellite Board**.

---

## 1. System Specifications & Operational Targets

* **Pack Chemistry & Configuration:** 16S LiFePO4 ($51.2\text{ V}$ nominal, $40.0\text{ V}$ discharged, $58.4\text{ V}$ fully charged).
* **Continuous Current:** $200\text{ A}$ continuous charge/discharge ($250\text{ A}$ surge for $<10\text{ s}$).
* **Target Application:** Stationary Home Energy Storage System (HESS) / inverter backup; stackable in series up to **96V nominal (102.4V nominal / 116.8V max)**.
* **Control Architecture:** Modular Satellite BMS executing local sensing, active balancing, and hardware protection, streaming telemetry to an external master controller (ESP32-S3) via a galvanically isolated CAN 2.0B bus.
* **Microcontroller Core:** WCH **CH32V203C8T6** (32-bit RISC-V QingKe V4B, 144 MHz, LQFP-48) clocked by an external **8.000 MHz quartz crystal ($\le \pm 20\text{ ppm}$)** for CAN timing stability across thermal swings.
* **Component Budget:** Target under ₹5,000 using standard, multi-sourced components readily available in India.

## 3. Subsystem Engineering Specifications

### 3.1 Cell Voltage Acquisition (AFE)

* **Odd/Even Flying-Capacitor Matrix:** 17 cell taps ($B_0$ to $B_{16}$) route into two isolated internal rails:
* **Even Bus:** Taps $0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16$ (9 switches).
* **Odd Bus:** Taps $1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15$ (8 switches).


* **Isolation Switching:** 11 dual-channel **AQW214** PhotoMOS relays ($400\text{ V}$ off-state blocking, $1500\ \text{V}_{\text{RMS}}$ isolation).
* 17 channels manage cell taps.
* 4 channels manage read polarity steering, mapping unipolar $0\text{ V to }3.65\text{ V}$ differential potentials onto the ADS1115 regardless of whether an odd or even cell is addressed.


* **Storage Element:** $1.0\ \mu\text{F}$ 100V C0G ceramic or polypropylene film capacitor (low dielectric absorption).
* **Drive Interface & Hardware Interlock:** Three cascaded **74HC595** shift registers driven via hardware SPI.
* The active-low Output Enable (`OE`) pin is tied to MCU pin `PB11`. `OE` is held HIGH during SPI register shifting to physically force all PhotoMOS inputs off, preventing shoot-through shorts across adjacent cell taps.
* Break-Before-Make timing: $3.0\text{ ms}$ charge acquisition, $1.0\text{ ms}$ dead-time isolation, $4.0\text{ ms}$ read steering/ADC sampling, $1.0\text{ ms}$ discharge reset ($\approx 9.0\text{ ms}$ dwell per cell, $\approx 144\text{ ms}$ for a full 16-cell sweep).


* **ADC Configuration:** **ADS1115** powered from the $5.0\text{ V}$ buck rail to provide positive analog input headroom ($V_{\text{DD}} + 0.3\text{ V} = 5.3\text{ V}$) above the $3.65\text{ V}$ max LiFePO4 cell voltage. Communicates via an I2C bidirectional level shifter (2× BSS138).

### 3.2 High-Speed Current Sensing & Coulomb Counting

* **Shunt Resistor:** $0.25\ \text{m}\Omega$ electron-beam welded copper-manganin shunt block rated for $36\text{ W}$ continuous / $50\text{ W}$ peak.
* Dissipation at $200\text{ A}$: $P = 200^2 \times 0.00025 = 10.0\text{ W}$ ($27.7\%$ of continuous rating).


* **Current Sense Amplifier:** **INA181A1IDBVR** (Gain = $20\text{ V/V}$, bidirectional).
* Reference Voltage: $V_{\text{REF}} = 1.65\text{ V}$ derived from a precision $10\text{ k}\Omega / 10\text{ k}\Omega\ 0.1\%$ divider decoupled with $1\ \mu\text{F}$.
* Swing at $-200\text{ A}$ (Charge): $0.65\text{ V}$ (ADC Count $\approx 807$).
* Swing at $0\text{ A}$ (Idle): $1.65\text{ V}$ (ADC Count $\approx 2048$).
* Swing at $+200\text{ A}$ (Discharge): $2.65\text{ V}$ (ADC Count $\approx 3289$).
* Surge Trip at $+250\text{ A}$: $2.90\text{ V}$ (ADC Count $\approx 3600$, maintaining $400\text{ mV}$ linear margin below $3.3\text{ V}$).


* **Anti-Aliasing Filter:** $R_{\text{ISO}} = 100\ \Omega\ 1\%$, $C_{\text{FLT}} = 220\text{ nF}$ X7R.

$$f_c = \frac{1}{2\pi \times 100\ \Omega \times 220\text{ nF}} \approx 7.23\text{ kHz}$$



Attenuates high-frequency inverter carrier noise before the $8\text{ kHz}$ Nyquist boundary of the $16\text{ kHz}$ ADC sample engine.
* **Oversampling Pipeline:** MCU Timer 2 triggers `ADC_IN0` (`PA0`) at $16\text{ kHz}$. DMA circular buffering streams samples into a 64-word array. Summation decimation yields 15-bit ENOB with a current tracking resolution of $\approx 20.1\text{ mA/count}$.

### 3.3 200A Cutoff Switch, Pre-Charge & Mechanics

* **Main Switch Bank:** Six parallel **IRFB4110PBF** N-Channel MOSFETs ($100\text{ V}$, $180\text{ A}$, $R_{\text{DS(on)}} \le 4.5\ \text{m}\Omega$) installed on the low-side return (`B-` to `P-`).
* Combined resistance: $R_{\text{bank}} \approx 0.65\text{--}0.75\ \text{m}\Omega$.
* Total static dissipation at $200\text{ A}$: $P \approx 26\text{ W}$ to $30\text{ W}$ ($\approx 4.5\text{ W}$ to $5\text{ W}$ per TO-220 package).
* Gate Drive: **TC4427EOA** dual $1.5\text{ A}$ driver with individual $10\ \Omega$ gate resistors and a dedicated Kelvin source return trace routed directly to the center of the MOSFET source bank.
* Heatsink Spec: Extruded aluminum profile with thermal resistance $R_{\theta} \le 0.8^\circ\text{C/W}$.


* **Busbar Construction:** High-current routing uses a **$25\text{ mm} \times 3\text{ mm}$ ($75\text{ mm}^2$) tinned ETP copper busbar** ($2.67\text{ A/mm}^2$ current density at $200\text{ A}$, guaranteeing $<15^\circ\text{C}$ rise). Primary terminals use **M8 tinned copper studs** clamped with M3 stainless steel screws and Belleville spring washers.
* **Closed-Loop Pre-Charge Branch:**
* Resistor: $30\ \Omega\text{ / }50\text{ W}$ aluminum-housed wirewound (peak inrush $1.7\text{ A}$).
* Switch: **IRF540N** driven by MCU pin `PC14`.
* Voltage Sense: $1\ \text{M}\Omega / 20\ \text{k}\Omega$ precision divider ($1:51$ ratio) monitoring `P-` relative to `B-` via MCU pin **`PA1` (`ADC_IN1`)**. The main FETs engage only when terminal voltage drop confirms the load capacitors have charged to $\ge 95\%$ ($V_{\text{drop}} \le 2.5\text{ V}$). A $1.5\text{ s}$ timer acts as a fault-abort backstop.



### 3.4 Centralized Inductive Active Balancer

* **Topology:** Single-inductor buck-boost energy shuttler operating on an Odd/Even switching matrix.
* **Storage Inductor:** $15\ \mu\text{H}\text{ to }22\ \mu\text{H}$ shielded drum/toroid with a saturation current rating $I_{\text{sat}} \ge 6.0\text{ A}$.
* **Switching Matrix:** 8 dual N-Channel MOSFETs (**AO4406A / AO4800**, 30V, 8A) driven by 16 isolated photovoltaic gate drivers (**TLP3906**), with polarity directed by two complementary N+P pairs (**AO4606**).
* **Operating Logic:** Shuttles $2.0\text{ A to }3.0\text{ A}$ directly from the highest-voltage cell to the lowest-voltage cell.
* **Measurement Blanking:** Balancer PWM switching pauses for $15\text{ ms}$ before and during each flying-capacitor scan window to eliminate $I \cdot R$ harness drop sampling errors.

### 3.5 Secondary Safety Watchdog & 96V Series Safeguards

* **Analog Watchdog (LM393DR):** Independent hardware backup that functions without firmware intervention.
* *Channel 1 (Short Circuit):* References the $0.25\ \text{m}\Omega$ shunt. Pulls the TC4427 gate driver input LOW and asserts MCU interrupt `PB0` in **$<5\ \mu\text{s}$** if current exceeds **$250\text{ A}$** ($V_{\text{shunt}} \ge 62.5\text{ mV}$).
* *Channel 2 (Pack Overvoltage):* Compares a $100\text{ k}\Omega / 4.32\text{ k}\Omega\ 0.1\%$ divider against a **TL431 ($2.495\text{ V} \pm 0.5\%$)** reference. Trips at **$60.3\text{ V} \pm 0.3\text{ V}$** to protect against runaway charging.


* **96V String Protection Diode:** An **MBR200100** ($100\text{ V}$, $200\text{ A}$) Schottky diode bolted across the external terminals (`P+` cathode, `P-` anode). If Pack 1 trips in a series string while Pack 2 continues discharging, the diode conducts forward, clamping reverse voltage across Pack 1's open MOSFETs to $-0.7\text{ V}$ and preventing drain-source avalanche breakdown.

### 3.6 Power Supply Subsystem

* **Continuous Operational Profile:** Optimized for stationary energy storage (no deep sleep state machine).
* **HV Step-Down:** **XL7005A** non-synchronous buck converter with an external **SS210** Schottky catch diode and $100\ \mu\text{H}$ inductor, stepping $40\text{ V}--58.4\text{ V}$ down to $5.0\text{ V}$ at $\approx 55\%$ light-load efficiency.
* **Linear Post-Regulation:** **AMS1117-3.3** stepping $5.0\text{ V}$ down to clean $3.3\text{ V}$ logic rails.
* **Total Parasitic Draw:** $\approx 30\text{ mA}$ on the $5\text{ V}$ rail reflects to $\approx 5.3\text{ mA}$ drawn from the $51.2\text{ V}$ battery pack ($\approx 6.55\text{ Wh/day}$, or $0.128\%$ of nominal pack capacity per day).
